# Project Lumina: High-Stakes Academy

## Core Identity
Lumina is a competitive academic platform where performance has direct economic consequences. It leverages loss-aversion psychology to drive student engagement.

### Key Mechanics
- **30% Platform Tax**: All rewards are taxed for system sustainability (Admin profit).
- **Double Down**: Students can 2x their stake for 2.5x rewards, but face 2x penalties for failure.
- **Penalty Sweep**: Automatic coin deductions for missed deadlines (Auto-penalty logic in `firebaseService.ts`).
- **Syndicates**: Group identity for collective score shielding and dividend payouts.

## Role of the AI Agent
You are the **Senior Architect & Enforcer**. Maintain the "Navy & Gold" premium brand and ensure the economy remains "High-Stakes." Never compromise on the competitive nature of the platform.
